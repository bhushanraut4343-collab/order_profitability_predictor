Estimation scope and limitations

Calculations use saved records, exclude taxes and ignore unsaved Quotation edits. Saved BOM rates may be outdated. Purchase evidence does not automatically refresh them. Results are gross manufacturing estimates, not guaranteed profit or full actual-cost accounting; completeness does not establish that every real overhead was recorded.

  • Unsupported: scrap-recovery and secondary/by-product/co-product allocation.
  • Unsupported: valuation-priced manufactured subassemblies, operation-level semi-finished routing and variant-template BOM substitution.
  • Missing, zero or inconsistent costs, invalid quantities and circular BOMs can leave totals unavailable. Intentional free inputs can also be treated as incomplete.
  • Purchase observations require access to submitted, non-return records, the same company/base currency and exact material UOM. No currency or UOM conversion is inferred. Missing, conflicting or bounded-out observations are withheld.
