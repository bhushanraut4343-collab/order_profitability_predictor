Information within your ERPNext site

The app reads relevant Quotations, BOMs, companies, items, operations, prices and permitted purchasing information to calculate manufacturing estimates and risks. It uses Frappe authentication and permissions.

Save Analysis stores source identifiers, financial summaries, item/component breakdowns, saved costing inputs, completeness, risks, timestamps and analyst user identifiers. Frappe supplies standard document ownership/audit metadata. New snapshots exclude live valuation purchase observations and related messages, but retain saved BOM costs and may retain other dated rate evidence. Existing snapshots are not automatically rewritten.

Application access depends on current roles, source records and financial-field permissions. Trusted server/database administrators can access underlying records. The app has no automatic snapshot expiry or ordinary user deletion workflow, and no dedicated personal-data export, redaction or erasure interface.

External communication and infrastructure

The reviewed app does not automatically transmit analysis data to the publisher or an external analytics service. Browser requests go to your Frappe application. Other installed apps, hosting services, logs and backups have separate behavior and policies. This statement does not promise that the entire deployment has no external communications or logs.

The app uses existing Frappe sessions; it adds no dedicated analytics or tracking storage. This website loads no external fonts, scripts, analytics or embedded third-party assets.

Support information received by the publisher

Personal Gmail (Google) processes support correspondence, sender details and diagnostic attachments. These are used to respond to requests and investigate reported issues. Use sanitized diagnostics by default. Explicit agreement is required before accepting sensitive financial information; agree on the minimum data and method first. Never send credentials or encryption keys.

Support retention and privacy requests

Publisher-controlled support information is retained only as reasonably needed for support and applicable legal, dispute or security purposes. Privacy requests will be handled as required by applicable law.

This wording does not remove statutory privacy duties. Requests may be sent to the contact above. Processing is limited to information reasonably needed for the stated purposes; retention exceptions do not justify indefinite storage.

Requests and deletion limits

Requests can be directed to the contact above. Identity and authority may require verification. Site-resident data requests also require the site operator; the publisher does not automatically have database access. Immutable financial analyses may require a supplementary correction record rather than an edit.

Deletion of publisher-controlled copies does not guarantee deletion from inaccessible Google backups or copies held by independent recipients. Google describes separate backend deletion and backup-retention timelines. Google retention information.

Backups and recovery

Uninstalling deletes the app’s snapshots; cancellation must not be assumed to do so. Reinstallation does not restore analyses. Copies can remain in site backups according to the operator’s retention policy. Full recovery can roll back unrelated records, and its success is not guaranteed.

Applicable privacy rights and obligations remain unaffected. This policy does not guarantee security outcomes or deletion from systems outside the publisher’s control.
